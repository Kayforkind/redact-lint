# Benchmark

Generated by `npm run bench`. Do not edit by hand.

> **What this is.** A synthetic corpus built at run time across several producers (pdf-lib, PDFKit, a hand-written content stream). It is not a scrape of published documents: it covers the shapes those documents take, and the gaps are listed below. Treat the numbers as a floor on synthetic cases, not as a real-world error rate.

## What the corpus covers

- painted covers: tight and loose boxes, black/white/grey, rotated page, two columns, three producers
- image covers pasted over text, and a page-sized scan that must not be flagged
- clean documents: zebra tables, dark header bars with white text, watermarks, a form, a blank page
- edge cases decided by the documented thresholds: partial coverage (under 60%, not reported), bars over empty space
- encrypted documents: excluded from the scores, asserted separately as an error path

## What it does not cover

- Word, LibreOffice, LaTeX and Acrobat output — not reproducible in this environment
- real scanned documents with OCR layers, CJK and right-to-left text
- partially transparent covers, clipping, blend modes
- documents larger than a few hundred pages

The gap that matters most is the first one. Adding real-world PDFs to `bench/corpus.mjs` is the highest-value contribution to this project.
